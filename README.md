## 🌷 About The Project

**Cherry Blossom Biome** is a Minecraft-inspired fantasy landscape built around the beauty of Japanese sakura trees and peaceful sunset scenery.

Imagine walking through a beautiful pink forest, listening to the flowing water, watching petals fall from the trees, and discovering a cozy little village beside a glowing lake.

A little world where everything feels peaceful.

## ✨ Features

- 🌸 Beautiful pink cherry blossom trees.
- 🏯 Japanese-inspired wooden architecture.
- 🌉 Lantern-lit wooden bridge.
- 💧 Flowing waterfall and reflective lake.
- 🏮 Warm glowing lanterns.
- 🌅 Magical sunset atmosphere.
- 🐈 Peaceful little details and flowers.
- 🌺 Dreamy pink and purple scenery.
